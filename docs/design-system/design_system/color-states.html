§ 03 · Color & States

Provenance is the system

Three groups — core, neutral, trust-states. Datum is ink on warm paper with a single red accent used sparingly for the active reference. The six trust states are the system's most important objects: every claim, signal and field carries one, read by color and by label.

§ 03.1

Core

Ink on warm paper. The datum-red accent is the active reference line — used sparingly, never decoratively. Concrete grey is the secondary / inactive tone.

§ 03.3

The six trust states

Fixed and semantic. Unknown is dashed (human verification required); Decision is ink-filled (arbitrated, frozen). Color never carries meaning alone — always pair with the label.

Sourced
#1F7A44

Adossé à une source citable. Référence + confiance.

Computed

Assumption

Decision
#16171A

Choix arbitré et figé. Ink, plein.
