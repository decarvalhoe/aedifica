§ 00.2

Principles

Six commitments. They decide every later question.

01 · Evidence over assertion

Nothing is stated without provenance. Every claim carries a state: sourced, computed, assumption, or unknown. We never hide what we don't know.

02 · Non-authority

We prepare; we do not rule. Language, layout and reports avoid any posture of legal authority or guaranteed outcome.

03 · Datum as structure

Reference lines, grids and dimension marks are the visual logic — not decoration. The page is a drawing, measured and aligned.

04 · States are first-class

The six trust states are designed objects with color and label — Sourced, Computed, Assumption, Unknown, Conflict, Decision.

05 · Controlled density

Dense where data lives, generous where decisions are made. Air is a tool, not a default.

06 · Radical, but useful

Post-classical Swiss precision in service of an office under deadline. No gradient magic, no blobs, no glass.
