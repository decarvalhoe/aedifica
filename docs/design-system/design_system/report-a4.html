§ 07 · Report A4

The sourced dossier

The A4 report is the most important deliverable AEDIFICA produces. It is print-first, evidence-led, and explicitly non-authority. Every conclusion is sourced; every gap is named as an unknown; the disclaimer is on every page.

Non-authority posture The report never states conformity, approval, or legal outcome as fact. It prepares sourced evidence and names what is unknown. Conclusions are phrased as preparation — the decision and conformity rest with the competent authority.
