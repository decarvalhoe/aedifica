§ 02 · Typography

Two families. One rule each.

Space Grotesk carries everything visible — display, UI, body. IBM Plex Mono appears for evidence annotations only. There is no third family.

§ 02.1

The lock

Non-negotiable. This is what makes the system itself.

UI & body

Aa

Space Grotesk 400 / 500

Evidence only

Aa

IBM Plex Mono 400 / 500

Forbidden IBM Plex Sans is banned anywhere in the system. Plex appears as Mono and only for annotations, refs, coordinates and source lines. Do not introduce a third sans, do not italicise the display weight, do not substitute Inter / Roboto / Arial.
