# MVP4 Spec — Direction Travaux Agentique

Issue: #19

## Goal

Support Swiss execution and construction-management workflows without taking contractual responsibility away
from the architect or direction de travaux.

## Inputs

- Site notes, photos, voice notes and meeting minutes.
- Contracts, tender assumptions, permit conditions and approved decisions.
- Companies, owners, deadlines, costs and planning milestones.
- Model/as-built links when available.

## Workflows

| Workflow | Output |
|---|---|
| Site notes/photos -> PV | Structured site report with evidence refs. |
| Minutes -> tasks | Owner, deadline, status, source sentence/photo. |
| Defect capture | Defect register, location, responsible party, close-out evidence. |
| Decision tracking | Site decision linked to source, actor and approval. |
| Cost/schedule signal | Warning when task/defect/decision affects budget or delay. |
| Handover | Reserves, warranties, manuals, as-built and final checklist. |

## Safety And Responsibility

- Aedifica can draft PVs and task lists; the responsible professional validates them.
- No automatic contractual commitment.
- Every task or defect needs source evidence.
- Every cost/schedule warning must show assumption and confidence.
- Open permit/compliance conditions must carry forward from phase `33`.

## Success Criteria

- Produces a clean PV from notes/photos.
- Extracts actionable tasks with owners/deadlines.
- Tracks defects and close-out evidence.
- Carries permit and tender assumptions into site/handover.
- Writes records into project memory for future operation or renovation.
